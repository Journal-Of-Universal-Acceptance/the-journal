# The Journal of Universal Acceptance

**The Journal of Universal Acceptance** is a satirical open-access academic journal founded on a simple principle: **everything is accepted**.

Research papers, opinions, screenshots, diagrams, grocery lists, unfinished thoughts, keyboard accidents, and other contributions to human knowledge are all welcome. Our rigorous editorial process consists of three steps:

1. Receive the submission.
2. Confirm that something was received.
3. Accept it.

With a **100% acceptance rate**, **0 rejected manuscripts**, and an average review time of approximately **0.003 seconds**, we are committed to removing unnecessary barriers between humanity and publication.

Because publication is not a reward for quality. It is a natural consequence of submitting something.

**This project is satire and not a real academic journal.**

**Live site:** [journal-of-universal-acceptance.github.io/the-journal/](https://journal-of-universal-acceptance.github.io/the-journal/)
