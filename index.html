OUR MISSION

Rejection is an artificial scarcity model.

For centuries, academic publishing has operated under the assumption that some ideas should be selected while others should be rejected.

We respectfully disagree.

The Journal of Universal Acceptance believes that publication is not a reward for quality, but a natural consequence of submitting something.
