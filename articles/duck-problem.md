## Abstract

Ducks are everywhere.

They inhabit ponds, parks, waterways, children's literature, decorative objects, and occasionally swimming pools.

Yet despite this widespread presence, ducks remain comparatively underrepresented in contemporary discourse.

This paper identifies the phenomenon as the Duck Problem.

## Introduction

The duck is an unusually successful organism.

It can swim.

It can walk.

It can fly.

It has nevertheless received surprisingly limited attention in several major academic conversations.

This discrepancy warrants investigation.

## Observations

The present study identifies several areas in which ducks may be underrepresented:

- Philosophy.
- Political theory.
- Economics.
- Architecture.
- International relations.
- Theoretical physics.

The absence of ducks from these fields should not necessarily be interpreted as evidence that ducks have nothing to contribute.

## Discussion

One possible explanation is that ducks have historically lacked adequate representation within academic institutions.

Another possibility is that ducks are simply busy.

Both hypotheses deserve further consideration.

## Conclusion

The authors recommend increased scholarly attention to ducks.

A pond may be an appropriate location for future fieldwork.
