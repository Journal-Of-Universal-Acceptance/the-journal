JOURNAL GOVERNANCE

Editorial Board

The Journal of Universal Acceptance is overseen by a distinguished editorial board representing a broad range of disciplines, methodologies, and opinions that we are prepared to accept.

EDITOR-IN-CHIEF

Prof. Reginald P. Puddifoot

Department of Applied Nonsense

Professor Puddifoot founded the Journal of Universal Acceptance after becoming concerned that too many journals were maintaining unnecessarily high standards for evidence.

His research interests include procedural ambiguity, administrative biscuits, and the statistical properties of things that are probably fine.

ASSOCIATE EDITOR

Dr. Mildred Biscuit

Centre for Domestic Phenomena

Dr. Biscuit specialises in the study of everyday occurrences that have somehow escaped formal academic investigation.

She is particularly interested in missing objects, unexplained noises, and the mysterious disappearance of pens from workplaces.

STATISTICAL EDITOR

Prof. Eustace Quibble

Institute for Quantitative Overthinking

Professor Quibble is responsible for ensuring that every article contains an appropriate number of statistics, regardless of whether statistics are necessary.

He has published extensively on margins of error, confidence intervals, and the confidence with which people make things up.

EDITORIAL BOARD MEMBER

Dr. Winifred P. Spoon

School of Experimental Domestic Science

Dr. Spoon's research concerns household mysteries, kitchen-based experimentation, and the epistemological limitations of asking someone where they put something.

She also serves as the journal's unofficial authority on refrigerator-related questions.

EDITORIAL BOARD MEMBER

Prof. Archibald N. Teaspoon

Department of Social Confusion

Professor Teaspoon researches the social consequences of forgotten names, awkward pauses, accidental waves, and saying "you too" when someone tells you to enjoy your meal.

He is currently investigating whether nodding can constitute peer review.

HONORARY EDITOR

The Anonymous Reviewer

Affiliation withheld

The Anonymous Reviewer has reviewed a significant proportion of submissions to the journal but has declined to provide a biography.

The editorial office respects this decision and has therefore invented several plausible details.

EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES

Our approach to peer review

The Journal of Universal Acceptance believes that knowledge should be shared freely and that unnecessarily restrictive publication standards discourage important contributions.

Submissions are therefore evaluated according to a flexible framework that prioritises enthusiasm, formatting, and the general impression that something has been written.

Where concerns arise during peer review, the editorial board generally resolves them by accepting the paper.
