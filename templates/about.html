ABOUT THE JOURNAL

The Journal of Universal Acceptance

The Journal of Universal Acceptance is a multidisciplinary publication devoted to the idea that the existence of a submission is, in itself, a compelling argument for its consideration.

We recognise the extraordinary range of human thought and believe that knowledge can take many forms. Research, observations, correspondence, lists, theories, questions, unfinished ideas, and other contributions all have the potential to become part of the published record.

Our editorial philosophy is based on a simple principle: participation should not be unnecessarily complicated by selection.

DISCLOSURE

A note about this project

This project was inspired by The Journal of Universal Rejection .

The Journal of Universal Acceptance is a satirical project. It is not a real academic journal, scholarly publisher, peer-review service, or accredited publication.

The acceptance policy, editorial process, journal metrics, and other institutional language used throughout this website are part of the joke.

Any articles or publications presented on this website should not be represented as peer-reviewed academic work or used to imply academic, scientific, or professional accreditation.
